How we work

Six clear stages, from first conversation to long-term support.

Consultation

We discuss what you need to power, your budget and your timing.

Site assessment

We check roof or ground space, shading and wiring, and list your appliances.

Design and proposal

A written proposal with the specification, components, price and terms.

Installation

Our team mounts the panels and installs the inverter, batteries and cabling.

Commissioning

We test under load, set up monitoring and show you how to use the system.

Support and upgrades

Maintenance, repairs and extra capacity as your needs grow.
